# Evidence to Action: classroom studio 2 publication

The owner authorized live website publication on October 25, 2026 and reported
teacher outreach underway. Outreach is not evidence of school-board approval,
a booked or paid pilot, curriculum mapping or measured programme impact.

- Teacher guide: 15 pages, `teachers/activity/evidence-to-action-teacher-guide.pdf`.

- Offline projection: 27 aligned slides in `teachers/activity/index.html`.
- Output checksums, sizes and counts: `data/classroom-publication.json`.

The core uses prepared AI exchanges and a complete paper route, with an
optional locally approved adult-operated demonstration. Students preserve an
initial view, brief the help, report its contribution, question and check claims,
iterate with evidence and defend a final decision. Agreed access supports remain
available. Independent transfer precedes substantive feedback and supported
revision; feedback is descriptive rather than an automatic score.

The teacher page and new T14 rationale identify the human skills being practised
and connect activities to EEF metacognition guidance (2025), IES learning guidance
(2007), the OECD Digital Education Outlook (2026) and UNESCO's student AI
framework (2024). They distinguish evidence-informed design from endorsement or
validation of this particular pilot. The optional delayed recall follow-up is
additional to the planned four-period core. Timings require educator dry runs.
