What to expect across devices

The apps share content and learning flows. Your nickname, progress, and Passport stay on each device: there is no account-based or cross-platform synchronization. The 3D worlds and native share controls can look different on iOS and Android.

Version 4.1 replaces earlier online Game Center play with local bot duels. The website-only Sandbox and guided programs remain separate from the apps. Earlier screenshots are explicitly labelled on the Campus and Learn pages.
