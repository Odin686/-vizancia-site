Prepare in about 10 minutes

One case. Evidence your learners can inspect.

1. Read and choose

Read the facilitator notes and the session-one garden case. Check your school’s approval, accessibility, and screen-use rules.

2. Print or project

The PDF includes worksheets and facilitation guidance. Use paper for individual answers. Save the projection page before class if you need it offline.

3. Ask for reasons

Ask each learner to name a source, a limitation, and a next check. Hear their reasons before revealing the discussion notes.

4. Return to the skill

Bring a new claim to the next session. Use the final transfer activity to see how learners apply the method in a different setting.

Your four-session sequence

  1. Follow the evidence

    Compare the garden claim with a small trial, an advertisement, and a groundskeeper’s note.

  2. A better prompt is not enough

    Inspect authored AI-style replies. Find the detail that sounds useful but has no supporting source.

  3. Make a decision together

    Weigh the evidence, affected people, and limits before recommending a next step.

  4. Use the method somewhere new

    Apply the checks to a fictional library display proposal. Explain what evidence would change your decision.

People, organisations, measurements, and AI-style replies in the activity are fictional. The slides do not generate responses or accept student input.
