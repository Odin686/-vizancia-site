Privacy at a glance

  • Core learning requires no Vizancia account and contains no in-app advertising. The iOS and Android 4.3 candidates include optional Google Analytics for Firebase, off by default and restricted to consenting adults using a personal device only for their own learning.
  • Lesson progress, XP, streaks, achievements, preferences, and any optional first name or nickname are stored locally on your device. Vizancia cannot retrieve or restore that local information.
  • Version 4.2 duels run against local bots. Profiles are device-local and do not sync between iOS and Android.
  • The website-only AI Sandbox is separate from the mobile App and has the online data flow described below.
  • If you contact us, including about a guided program, we receive the contact information and content you choose to send.
  • This Website loads Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion measurement only after you choose “Accept measurement” in the website notice. Until then, no Google script runs and no cookies are set. No personalised advertising is used. Hosting providers still process requests and security logs, as explained below.
  • Vizancia does not sell personal information or use App learning data for advertising.

This summary is for convenience. The complete Policy governs.

1. Scope and accountability

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) explains how Vizancia Incorporated (“Vizancia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), an Ontario, Canada corporation, handles personal information in connection with the Vizancia mobile application (the “App”), vizancia.com and its AI Learning Hub (the “Website”), support communications, and related services.

Vizancia is the organization responsible for personal information under its control and the controller or equivalent where applicable. Our Privacy Officer is accountable for this Policy and can be reached using the contact information in Section 12.

This branded page is the public Privacy Policy for both mobile apps and this Website. The reviewed iOS and Android 4.3 (13) candidates have not been uploaded or submitted to either store. The last recorded public store version is 4.2; see dated platform release details. Firebase disclosures apply to builds that include the SDK; installing it alone does not grant consent.

The App, Website, app stores, and connected services do not all use data in the same way. This Policy separates those practices so that a person can understand what changes before choosing a connected feature.

2. Core App and on-device information

The core App does not require a Vizancia account. The App offers an optional first name or nickname during onboarding; it can be left blank and is stored only on the device. We do not ask for an email address, phone number, password, advertising identifier, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health information, or payment-card details to use core lessons and games.

The App stores the following information locally on the device so that it can provide progress and personalization:

Learning progressLesson completion, quiz scores, XP, levels, streaks, and achievements.
PreferencesApp settings, selected options, notification preferences, and similar device-local choices.
Reflections and practiceOptional Passport writing, self-assessments, practice answers, feedback ratings, local profile labels, and activity history. Keep private information out of shared-device work.
Optional profile labelA first name or nickname entered during onboarding. Leaving the field blank uses a generic “Learner” label.

Vizancia does not receive this on-device progress. On iOS, device backups may include App data under Apple’s terms. Android automatic app backup is disabled in the reviewed 4.3 candidate; device transfers and external copies can still depend on the operating system and device settings. There is no Vizancia cloud account or cross-platform synchronization. Deleting the App or clearing its data generally removes the local copy, although a platform backup can remain until managed through the relevant platform account.

Reminder notifications, when enabled, are scheduled locally. Vizancia does not receive the notification schedule or content.

Optional app Firebase analytics

The following text is reproduced from the reviewed iOS app policy. The Android 4.3 candidate uses the same eligibility, data exclusions, retention and withdrawal rules. Platform storage differences are explained below. Website consent, the adult reading check, and selecting a learning stage do not enable app analytics.

When optional Firebase analytics can run

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand basic app usage and improve navigation and reliability. It is off by default for every learner. Settings requires a separate confirmation that you are 18 or older and use the device only for your own learning, not for children, students or other learners.

  • Onboarding must be complete
  • The selected grade band must be high school or the corresponding adult level
  • There must be exactly one local learner profile
  • Classroom Mode must be off
  • An adult must explicitly confirm personal use and allow analytics

A grade-band choice does not establish age or grant consent. Younger bands, multiple profiles and Classroom Mode exclude collection. The app does not configure Firebase on a cold launch without eligible saved consent.

Information Google receives after opt-in

When eligible analytics is enabled, the SDK sends usage and technical information to Google. This may include:

  • A generated app-instance identifier that groups activity from that app installation
  • Basic lifecycle and engagement events, such as first launch, sessions and engagement duration
  • Screen-view events for the fixed tab names Play, Learn, Train, Campus and Progress
  • App identifier and version, device model, operating system, language and related device or app metadata
  • Event times and approximate location derived from network information

An app-instance identifier is pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity. Network requests expose an IP address to Google for delivery and processing. Google states that Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses. Analytics does not use the device's GPS or Apple's Location Services.

Google Analytics data safeguards

Information excluded from analytics

Our analytics interface accepts fixed navigation labels, not free text or learning records.

  • No nickname, email address, local profile ID or grade band
  • No lesson answers, scores, XP, game results, review cards or learning histories
  • No AI Passport text, project notes, written responses or workshop code and test traces
  • No lesson-feedback ratings, contact lists, photos, microphone recordings or precise location
  • No account user ID, advertising identifier or vendor identifier

The app denies advertising storage, advertising user-data and advertising-personalization consent. Advertising-ID support, vendor-ID collection and automatic screen reporting are disabled. The current release does not use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging or Firebase AI services.

Your analytics choices and withdrawal

You may leave analytics off or withdraw consent in Settings. Withdrawing stops collection and resets local analytics data and identifiers. The app records a local withdrawal marker before attempting to save the profile preference, so a failed preference save does not restore collection on this device.

  • Switching profiles, adding a second learner, choosing a younger band or entering Classroom Mode stops collection and resets local analytics data
  • Those exclusions suspend a saved choice; the same learner's choice may resume when the app becomes eligible again
  • Settings offers withdrawal of a saved choice when it is suspended; leave a temporary class session to manage personal settings if needed
  • Reset All Progress keeps preferences, including the analytics choice, and does not withdraw consent
  • Only a newly confirmed and successfully saved opt-in clears a withdrawal marker

Withdrawal applies to future collection. It does not automatically delete information already received by Google. Reinstalling, clearing app data or resetting a local identifier is also not a request to delete previously sent information. Contact us about that information as explained below.

Retention and deletion

Local learning records stay until you delete the relevant records or profile, reset progress, or remove the app. Some identity and preference settings survive Reset All Progress. Backups, exported files and recovery copies have separate lifetimes.

  • As reviewed on October 17, 2026, the app's Google Analytics property retains event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months
  • Reset on new user activity is enabled, so new activity can extend the user identifier's 14-month retention window
  • These settings do not impose the same expiry on standard aggregated reports
  • Google's deletion processing and operational retention follow its applicable service terms; deletion is not instantaneous
  • Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and related legal or administrative obligations; you may ask us to delete it

We review retention settings when changing analytics practices. A request to delete local progress does not delete analytics already sent, and an analytics deletion request does not erase records held only on your device.

Children, families and schools

The learning app supports younger audiences with adult guidance. Optional analytics is not offered for children's or students' learning and must not be enabled on a device used for them. Adults should use temporary Classroom Mode for shared sessions and review local profiles before handing a device to another person.

A school or educator must follow its own device, privacy, sharing and approval rules. Using the app or its adult check does not establish school approval or verified parental consent. If you believe a child's information was sent to us, contact info@vizancia.ca without including the child's work or unnecessary identifying details.

Reports and information you choose to share

An adult may review a report and use Apple's share sheet to select a destination. Learning Reports and Class Period summaries can include a nickname, grade or module and practice results. Beta feedback reports contain structured lesson results and ratings; those details may identify a learner in context.

AI Passport text and other written responses are excluded from those reports and analytics. Review the full report before sharing and follow school rules. We receive a report only if you choose to send it to us. Copies you share, screenshots and text you copy are controlled by you and the recipients.

Backups, recovery copies and device removal

Apple or computer backups may contain local app data depending on device settings. If a database cannot open, the app may preserve a recovery copy on the device before starting a fresh store. Removing the app does not erase external backups, recovery copies outside its container or files already shared.

Vizancia does not operate a cloud learning-sync service and cannot remotely retrieve or erase learning records held only on your device. Device owners and school administrators manage backups and shared copies through their own systems.

Android storage and sharing

Android uses app-private WebView storage for local learning records and independent local withdrawal markers. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in the reviewed 4.3 candidate. Android reports use the system share sheet. Exported reports, screenshots, device transfers and other external copies can have separate lifetimes. A damaged or newer save is protected rather than silently replaced. The Apple backup paragraph above applies to iOS.

3. Connected services and information you provide

Classroom sessions, reports, and sharing

Classroom Mode in version 4.2 uses a temporary session separate from the personal profile. Session work clears when the session ends, times out, the app backgrounds, or the app restarts. Personal profiles remain on the device. Local profiles are not password protected. The iOS and Android 4.3 adult check asks someone helping to follow a reading instruction. It deters accidental actions; it is not a password, identity check or age verification. Ordinary personal Class Period results can persist and are different from temporary Classroom Mode.

An adult can choose to export a progress report, completion PDF, or feedback report using the platform share sheet. Reports may contain a nickname, grade band, and learning results; report types disclose their contents before sharing. Passport reflections are not included in the classroom progress report. Shared copies are controlled by the selected recipient or service and are not removed by deleting App data. External AI-tool links open third-party services under their own policies; core lessons do not send prompts to a live model.

Support and correspondence

If you email us, submit a report, inquire about a guided program, or otherwise contact us, we receive the email address or contact details you use, the message and attachments you send, and any organization, household, country or region, group-size, timing, device, operating-system, or App-version details you choose to include. We use that information to respond, recommend a suitable next step, prepare a scope or proposal, troubleshoot, investigate safety or legal issues, maintain records, and protect the Services.

The Programs inquiry form is email-based. Its answers remain in the browser until the visitor asks the device to prepare an email addressed to info@vizancia.ca. Vizancia does not receive those answers unless the visitor sends the prepared message through their email provider. The email provider then processes the message under its own terms and privacy practices.

Website-only AI Sandbox limited beta

The AI Sandbox is a Website experience and is not included in the offline mobile App. If you use the Sandbox, the prompt you submit, the generated response, and limited technical request information must be processed online to provide the response and apply safety and security controls. Platform services

Version 4.2 uses local bot duels and does not integrate Game Center or online player matching. Earlier iOS releases offered optional adult-profile Game Center features, governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

Apple and Google independently process App Store and Google Play data. Vizancia may receive aggregated store reports, ratings and reviews, and platform-provided diagnostic reports, depending on the relevant store and user settings. We do not receive payment-card details from the stores. Review Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Website hosting and measurement

Website and App choices are separate. Accepting measurement on this Website enables the website measurement described in this section. It does not enable analytics inside the mobile App or give us access to your device-local learning progress. A website visit or app-store click does not confirm that the App was installed or opened.

The public Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not advertising measurement is used. See GitHub’s hosting explanation and privacy statement. Hosting security logs are separate from the optional measurement described next.

What we measure and when. The Website uses Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads conversion measurement, provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors find the Website and whether they continue to the App Store or Google Play. These tools run in Google’s consent mode and are off by default. Until you choose “Accept measurement” in the website notice, no Google measurement script is loaded, no measurement cookies are set, no store click is recorded, and no measurement data is sent to Google. If you accept, the Website loads the Google tag and sets first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*, _gcl_au) for up to 13 months to count visits, sessions, engaged visits after ten seconds with the page visible, and clicks to the app stores. Google receives page URLs, referrers, browser and device details, and technical request information such as an IP address, along with measurement identifiers. Google Ads may use a click identifier from an ad you clicked to attribute that visit to the ad.

What we do not do. We do not use personalised advertising, remarketing audiences, or Google Signals on this Website, and personalised-advertising signals are disabled in the tag configuration. We do not send Google your name, email address, or the content of any message you send us. A website visit, an app-store click, and a completed app installation are different events; we do not treat store clicks as installs.

Your choices. Choosing “Essential only” keeps measurement off. You can change your choice at any time using the “Website privacy” button at the bottom of every page; withdrawing acceptance disables Analytics, removes the first-party Google cookies the Website can control, and reloads the page to unload the Google tag. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the Website treats it as a refusal: no measurement script is loaded, even if an earlier acceptance was saved. We remember either choice for 180 days in your browser’s local storage under vizancia_consent_v2, then ask again; a preference saved before September 13, 2026 is removed and is not reused.

History. An earlier consent-based Google Ads tag was removed on September 13, 2026. Measurement was reinstated on September 14, 2026 in the opt-in form described above. Google’s handling of measurement data is described in “How Google uses information from sites that use its services”.

Withdrawing acceptance or refusing measurement does not delete information Google already received from an earlier accepted visit, or cookies your browser already holds from other sites. Use your browser’s storage controls and Google’s privacy information for that data.

Links to Apple, Google, external tools, and email services take you to those providers. A website visit, app-store click, and completed app installation are different events; we do not treat store clicks as installs.

5. Purposes and legal bases

We process the limited information described above to:

  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and enforce the Services;
  • investigate safety, abuse, fraud, infringement, or legal reports;

We do not use personal information for solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

  • operate a user-selected connected feature through a contracted service provider;
  • use app-store and operating-system services selected by the user;
  • obtain professional advice, maintain security, or investigate a report; or
  • respond to a valid legal process, protect rights or safety, or complete a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

Service providers are required by contract or applicable platform rules to handle information only for authorized purposes and with protections appropriate to the information. No provider can make an electronic service risk-free.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Core App progress: remains on the device until the user clears App data or removes the App, subject to platform backups controlled by the user and platform provider.
  • Support, program, and legal correspondence: kept only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, prepare or administer a requested engagement, maintain appropriate business or legal records, resolve disputes, and protect the Services, then deleted or de-identified under our records practices.
  • Sandbox: Vizancia does not provide persistent conversation history. Provider-side handling and deletion details are disclosed in the participant notice before the beta is enabled.
  • App Firebase analytics: the shared disclosures above describe the recorded October 17 property-settings review: 2-month event retention, 14-month user retention, reset on new activity, aggregated-report exceptions, and the difference between withdrawal and server-side deletion. These app settings do not establish website-property retention.
  • Website measurement: the first-party Google measurement cookies last up to 13 months. Analytics cookie expiry is not extended on each visit. Refusing or withdrawing measurement clears the cookies the Website can control; your saved consent choice is valid for 180 days. Google controls retention of measurement data it has already received under its policies and the relevant account settings. GitHub controls its hosting security logs under its privacy practices.

Where we must retain information for law, security, fraud prevention, or a legal claim, we restrict it to those purposes for the required period.

8. Children's privacy

Vizancia is an educational product that may be used by children with appropriate adult involvement. The core App requires no account, contains no in-app advertising and keeps learning progress and optional nicknames local. The iOS and Android 4.3 candidates exclude Firebase collection in younger grade bands, with multiple learner profiles, and in Classroom Mode. Analytics must not be enabled on a device used for children, students or other learners. A high-school setting does not verify adulthood; a separate 18+ own-personal-learning declaration is required. No online player matching is offered.

The website-only AI Sandbox is an adult-managed limited beta and is not authorized for independent use by a child under 13. A parent, legal guardian, or authorized educator must review the feature notice, supervise use, and complete any consent process required by law. The public Website loads Google measurement only after an explicit “Accept measurement” choice in the website notice, with personalised advertising disabled. The classroom projection view has no student input form or live AI endpoint.

If we learn that personal information was collected from a child in a way that requires parental consent and valid consent was not obtained, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. A parent or guardian can contact the Privacy Officer to ask whether information associated with a child is held, request access or deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent.

9. Privacy rights and choices

Depending on location and subject to legal exceptions, a person may have rights to know or access personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion or portability, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, appeal a decision, and receive equal service without unlawful discrimination for exercising a right.

Vizancia does not operate a cloud learner database. An opted-in iOS or Android installation can have pseudonymous analytics records at Google. We do not send local profile IDs or account IDs and may be unable to associate particular analytics records with you. We will explain technical or legal limits when handling a request. Device-local records can be managed in the App or through device storage controls; backups and shared copies are separate. Reset All Progress preserves analytics preferences and is not a withdrawal or server-side deletion request. The “Website privacy” button lets you review or change your website measurement choice. Other requests can be sent to the Privacy Officer. We may need to verify a request proportionately before acting.

Canada

Privacy rights and obligations depend on applicable Canadian federal and provincial law, including PIPEDA and Quebec private-sector privacy law where they apply. A person may complain to Vizancia and, where applicable, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or a provincial regulator.

California and other U.S. states

We do not sell personal information. We do not knowingly use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics. Some laws may define particular advertising-technology disclosures as “sharing” even without payment. The Website loads measurement only after an explicit acceptance, never builds personalised-advertising audiences, and treats a Global Privacy Control signal as a refusal even if an earlier acceptance was saved.

EEA and United Kingdom

Where applicable, a person may exercise GDPR or UK GDPR rights and lodge a complaint with a local supervisory authority. Consent can be withdrawn without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

10. Security, incidents, and international transfers

We use data minimization, access controls, encrypted transport for connected requests, provider review, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the limited information under our control. No transmission, storage system, or automated filter is completely secure.

Connected-service and hosting information may be processed in Canada, the United States, and other countries where providers operate. Where required, we use contractual or other lawful transfer safeguards. Platform data handled independently by Apple or Google is governed by their safeguards.

If a breach of safeguards creates a risk that triggers notification or reporting duties, we will investigate, document, notify affected people, and report to regulators as required by applicable law.

11. Browser signals, third-party links, and changes

The App does not track users across other companies' apps or websites. The Website honours Global Privacy Control as described above. We do not rely on the older “Do Not Track” signal because it is not interpreted consistently.

Third-party links and services are governed by their own terms and policies. Vizancia is not responsible for a third party's independent practices.

We may update this Policy to reflect product, provider, legal, or operational changes. We will revise the date and version on this page and provide additional notice or obtain renewed consent for material changes where required. A Policy update does not replace a consent the law requires.
